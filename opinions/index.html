Writing

Occasional notes
I.

Beli is the wrong shape for restaurants.

Ranking by ordinal pairwise comparison works for songs because you listen on demand. Restaurants you visit once a year if you're lucky, and the rank that matters is conditional — best Thai under $25 in Oakland tonight — not absolute. A general top-N list of restaurants is a leaderboard of nostalgia, not a recommendation. The product that wins this category isn't Beli; it's a list builder that takes constraints as first-class input.

II.

Every working engineer should run a Claude bot from their phone.

I run one. It is the single largest delta in my throughput in five years. The reason is not that the model is smart — it is that the loop closes while I'm in line for coffee. Plan on the walk, push before lunch. The orthodoxy that "real engineering" requires a chair and a monitor is a memory of a tool that no longer exists. If you haven't tried it, you are budgeting against a price that has already moved.

III.

Group chats are pull, not push.

Treating a group chat like email — checking it on a schedule, replying when you have something to say — is correct. Treating it like a notification stream that demands real-time attention is what makes people leave them. The conversational primitive is older than the software; it survives only if you let it move at human pace.

IV.

Spotify hasn't shipped a good feature in five years.

Discover Weekly was the last one that mattered. Everything since has been either a UI churn, a podcast pivot the user didn't ask for, or an AI DJ feature that nobody requested and nobody uses twice. The company is sitting on the world's best listening-behavior dataset and has produced no novel use of it in half a decade. That's not execution; that's complacency.

V.

A personal site without opinions is a résumé in disguise.

If you're not willing to be wrong on the internet under your own name, you don't have a personal site — you have a landing page. The point of a domain you own is to say a thing you wouldn't say in a Slack channel. If three years from now I read this and cringe, it was worth writing. If I still agree with every word, I was too cautious.
