How it works

  1. Pick a plan and tell me a bit about your business. I get a notification immediately.
  2. I reach out within 24 hours to confirm and collect the details (logo, photos, hours, menu or services).
  3. First draft within a week. You tell me what to change. I ship the fix same day.
  4. Site goes live. If you signed up for review auto-reply, we connect your Google Business Profile in a 5-minute walkthrough.
  5. Cancel anytime. No hostage-holding your domain or your site.

About the review auto-reply

Every Google review gets a thoughtful, human-sounding response within a few hours. Positive reviews get thanked in your voice. Negative reviews get acknowledged and addressed, no corporate-speak, no legal liability.

Default is we draft, you approve in one tap. You can enable auto-post for lower-stakes positive reviews. You fill out a short onboarding form (how you normally talk, what to mention, how to handle 1-stars) and we stick to it.
