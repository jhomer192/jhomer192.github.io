About Jack Homer

Jack Homer is a Forward Deployed Engineer at C3 AI in Redwood City, California, where he is a technical lead across U.S. Marine Corps enterprise AI programs. He builds production multi-agent systems and agentic developer tooling, and he holds a B.S. in computer science from Virginia Tech.

In short

  • What he does

    Forward-deployed work: sitting with a customer, turning a written specification into a working system, and staying with it through production. At C3 AI that has meant workforce and logistics applications for the Marine Corps, a multi-agent translation system built to a court deadline, and an agentic harness that carries development from a ticket to a reviewed pull request.

  • What he builds outside work

    A free system-design interview trainer, a local language model that plays Pokémon unassisted, a puzzle builder whose entire database is the link you share, and a job-application agent that reads a posting and fills the form. They are catalogued as a star chart on this site.

  • Where he studied

    Virginia Tech, College of Engineering, B.S. computer science, cum laude.

  • Where he is

    The San Francisco Bay Area.
