Records

Ten albums I keep coming back to, in no particular order.
α

Hand Habits — Blue Reminder

Quiet, watchful songwriting that builds without raising its voice. The record I've reached for most when I needed to think.

β

MJ Lenderman — Manning Fireworks

A guitar record that earns its slack-jawed swagger. "She's Leaving You" is the kind of song that makes you forgive a whole genre.

γ

Mk.gee — Two Star & the Dream Police

Production as identity. Every record this decade will be measured against how confidently it commits to a sound; this one commits hardest.

δ

Adrianne Lenker — Bright Future

The closest thing to a fireside record anyone made this year. Plain instruments, unflinching writing.

ε

Charli XCX — Brat

Pop record of the decade so far. Earned every meme, and the meme economy did not exhaust the album — the album is still better than the discourse.

ζ

Vampire Weekend — Only God Was Above Us

The band finally writing like adults without losing the texture. The string arrangements are doing actual work.

η

Magdalena Bay — Imaginal Disk

Concept-album commitment in a year that mostly produced singles. Holds together cover-to-cover, which is a feat.

θ

Geordie Greep — The New Sound

The most insane record on this list and I won't be talked out of it. Lounge-music horror; jazz as social pressure.

ι

Waxahatchee — Tigers Blood

Plainspoken country-adjacent songwriting at the height of its powers. Plays best in a car on a Sunday.

κ

The Cure — Songs of a Lost World

A late record that earns its weight. Slow, monumental, exactly as long as it needs to be — which is to say, longer than you'd let almost any other band get away with.
