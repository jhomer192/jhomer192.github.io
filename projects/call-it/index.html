Call It

prediction leagues for friends
Side project · 2026 · Live, phone sign-up

Prediction markets for a group of friends, played with points instead of money. Start a league, invite people by link, post a question with two to ten possible answers, and trade shares in each answer until an admin settles it. Signing up takes a US phone number.

Call It turns a friend group's running bets, such as who gets engaged first or whether the flight will be delayed, into a market. Somebody posts the question with its possible answers, everyone gets the same pile of points, and the price of each answer moves as people buy and sell it.

With a market maker behind every bet, you never need someone to take the other side; you just buy what you think is underpriced, and the number on the screen becomes the group's live estimate of the odds.

How it works

  • Prices move with the trading: new bets run on a logarithmic market scoring rule (LMSR), so buying an answer raises its price and every price reads as the group's current odds. A share pays one point if its answer wins. You get a quote before you trade and can sell back at any time.
  • League admin: the owner picks a starting balance from 100 to 100,000 points; admins close, reopen, resolve or void bets, and a void refunds everyone; members can be removed and restored.
  • Seasons: ending one voids the open bets, saves the standings and awards, and starts everyone fresh. The leaderboard ranks players by net worth.
  • Live updates: trades and settlements appear on every open screen as they happen. Invites go out by link, QR code or username search.
  • No real money anywhere. Points cannot be bought, sold or cashed out.

Built with

React 19 · Vite · a plain Node server · Postgres on Supabase, with Supabase phone sign-in (password plus an SMS code). Self-hosted behind Caddy. An iPhone build is in progress.

Open to anyone with a US phone number Sign up, start your own league, and invite friends by link. To join someone else's league you need their invite.
