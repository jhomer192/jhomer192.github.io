Sticker Maker

cut-out stickers from any photo
Side project · 2026

Cut anything out of a photo and turn it into a sticker. Tap the subject and a model running on your phone lifts it out; brush, lasso and wand tools fix the edges. Copy or share it anywhere, or export files sized for Discord, Telegram or WhatsApp. No account.

Sticker Maker is a web page. Open it, pick a photo, tap the thing you want, and the background goes away. The editor is there for the cases the model gets wrong: hair, a hand in front of a face, a subject the same colour as the wall.

It is a newer, bigger take on Polygon Stickers, which only targets the iOS sticker drawer. This one keeps a library, exports files sized for several chat apps, and lets you share whole packs.

What it does

  • The cut-out runs on your device: MediaPipe's interactive segmenter, self-hosted and downloaded only the first time you use it, takes a tap on the subject and returns a mask.
  • Tools to fix the mask: lift, brush, lasso and a colour wand, plus text, crop and a zoomed precision mode. The photo and the mask are stored separately, so any edit can be redone later without losing quality.
  • Import from a screenshot: point it at a screenshot of a sticker drawer and it pulls out every sticker in it.
  • Exports sized for each app: Discord emoji and stickers, 512-pixel Telegram stickers and WhatsApp, saved as image files or sent through the share sheet. Adding them to the chat app is still a step you do there.
  • Packs to share: publish a pack and you get a link. A public pack shows up in Explore only after review, and anyone can report one.
  • Private by default: editing happens on the phone, and nothing is uploaded until you choose to publish.

Built with

React 19 · TypeScript · Vite · MediaPipe in the browser; Node 24 with node:sqlite behind Caddy for shared packs. A native iPhone version with an iMessage sticker extension is in progress.

Open to anyone No sign-up. Stickers are saved in your browser, so export your library from Settings if you want a copy.
