Forward Deployed Engineer at C3 AI

Technical Lead, Federal Enterprise AI · Redwood City, California · 2024–present

I lead delivery of production AI for federal and enterprise customers: technical lead and primary developer across $10M+ in total contract value of U.S. Marine Corps programs, three concurrent teams, and a run of internal systems that started as one engagement's problem and became reusable across the portfolio.

Selected projects

  • US Marine Corps, Enterprise AI Programs

    Technical lead across Manpower & Reserve Affairs and Marine Expeditionary Force personnel and logistics programs, leading three concurrent teams of roughly nine engineers. Took MEF G-1 from a 108-page specification to production on the C3 AI Platform, deployed on Advana, the Department of War data platform operated by CDAO. Ran on-site workshops with stakeholders from end users up to command-level leadership.

  • Multi-agent legal translation, English to Italian

    Shipped a translation system on a hard court deadline for a $2.3B intellectual-property case: an OCR pipeline, a translator–critic–arbitrator loop that scores and reconciles output, and a ten-agent verification swarm. It handled 1,081 documents, 2.2 million words, at $2.31 per document against a six-figure outside-vendor quote.

  • Agentic coding harness

    Built a production harness that drives development asynchronously from a ticket to a reviewed pull request: it generates code, runs build, unit and end-to-end tests, and opens a PR per work unit behind an automated senior-tier review gate. Models are tiered — cheap for orchestration, frontier for the gates — holding the cost under $10 in model spend per work unit. Used it to auto-complete tickets and to power a multi-agent UI migration adopted by several teams.

  • Access-control verification

    Engineered a reusable method for verifying role-based access control, exercising both backend and interface as each role against the written permission matrix. It verified 176 role-by-capability combinations on a federal case-management system before go-live, and was packaged as a portable security-testing tool for other engagements.

  • Data Asset Management Framework

    Designed a data-ingestion framework that cuts onboarding a new external data source into a C3 application to roughly half a day of developer time, driven by a natural-language prompt and a config-driven connector setup. The interface is fully dynamic, so a new source needs little backend work, and the whole thing ships as a package any project can adopt.

  • Law-enforcement operating system, prototype

    Built a working prototype for a county law-enforcement agency in 48 hours on C3 Code: search-warrant generation matching the department's exact format, dynamic dashboards, geospatial mapping, PII redaction, and entity link analysis. Demonstrated live to county police chiefs ahead of a renewal.

  • Internal AI enablement

    Led the AI SWAT team, C3's internal group building AI tooling across the company, and served as an internal AI advisor with the President routing vice presidents and department heads to me for enablement. Also built a spec-to-design pipeline: a structured prompt distilled from subject-matter-expert interviews turns a program brief into a product spec, then an autonomous four-hour design run produces a full clicked-through application front end.

  • Platform reliability

    Sole customer-facing engineer on the company-wide platform-stability tiger team. Built crash-triage tooling that traces the system actions preceding a crash, led the field team's triage, and published internal guides on the methodology, as developer-impacting crashes fell from roughly ninety a day to about five.
